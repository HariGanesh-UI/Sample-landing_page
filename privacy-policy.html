About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildersNova, operated by ATTNS INNOVATION LABS PVT LTD ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects your personal and business-related information.

BuildersNova is a construction and project management platform designed to help builders, contractors, developers, project managers, site engineers, supervisors, accountants, purchase managers, store keepers, and other authorized personnel manage construction projects and related operations through a centralized platform.

The Platform may provide features including project and site management, daily and weekly progress reporting, material and inventory management, material indents, purchase orders, vendor management, payment tracking, user and role management, sales information, dashboards, reports, and other construction-management functionalities.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and handled when you access or use the BuildersNova application, website, or related services.

We prioritize the protection of your personal and business information and implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

By accessing or using BuildersNova or providing information through the Platform, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and our applicable Terms of Use. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should refrain from using the Platform or providing personal information.
Section I

Collection of Information

How Information is Collected

We may collect information directly from you when you:

  • Create or access a BuildersNova account.
  • Register an organization, builder, contractor, project, or construction site.
  • Add or manage employees, users, vendors, or other authorized personnel.
  • Create or update project and site information.
  • Submit daily or weekly progress reports.
  • Create material indents, purchase orders, stock movements, or inventory records.
  • Enter vendor, supplier, payment, or transaction information.
  • Use dashboards, reports, sales modules, or other Platform features.
  • Contact our support team.
  • Submit feedback or communicate with us.
  • Access the Platform through a mobile device, browser, or other supported device.

We may also automatically collect certain information through cookies, server logs, analytics tools, and similar technologies when you access or use the Platform.

What Information is Collected

Depending on your role and use of the Platform, we may collect:
